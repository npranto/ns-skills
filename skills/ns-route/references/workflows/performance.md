### performance

```
discover (scoped) ─▶ mp:diagnosing-bugs (regression) | mp:research (unfamiliar platform behavior)
   ─▶ spec? ◆ ─▶ [TICKET] (implement: benchmark before and after; test: load/performance type)
```

- `spec` only at medium scope or larger.
